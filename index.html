ABOUT US

Smiles for the Whole Family

At T-REX Dental Clinic, we believe that a healthy smile is the foundation of confidence and well-being. As a multispeciality dental clinic, we bring together highly qualified specialists from every field of dentistry — all under one roof — to provide complete, personalized care for you and your family.

We are committed to delivering the highest quality dental treatment with a strong focus on hygiene, safety, and comfort. By combining advanced modern technologies with gentle care, we ensure every patient receives the best possible treatment experience.

Whether it’s preventive care, advanced dental procedures, or creating beautiful smiles, our goal is to make every visit comfortable, reliable, and result-oriented. At T-Rex Dental Clinic, your smile is our priority.

Dr. NARINDERJEET SINGH

BDS, HPGDC, IGMC- Shimla

Dr. Narinderjeet Singh, BDS, is a dedicated dental professional and the Managing Director of T-REX Dental Clinic. He completed his Bachelor of Dental Surgery in 2022 from HPGDC, IGMC-Shimla. With a vision to provide advanced, hygienic, and patient-friendly care, he established T-REX Dental Clinic as a multispeciality center equipped with the latest technology.

Dr. RITIKA SHARDA

BDS, MDS (Prosthodontics and Crown & Bridge)

Dr. Ritika Sharda is a skilled dental professional who completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) in 2019 and earned her Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Prosthodontics and Crown & Bridge in 2023. She specializes in smile restoration, dental crowns, bridges, and implant-supported prostheses, helping patients regain function, aesthetics, and confidence.
